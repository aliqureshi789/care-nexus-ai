# Workflow Approval Policy

Reference: POL-APP-001

Summary: Requests with complete documentation and no escalation triggers may proceed for approval.

## Description

This policy governs the approval of enterprise workflow requests across departments. All requests must be reviewed against completeness, accuracy, and compliance criteria before approval.

## Rules

- All mandatory documentation must be provided before approval.
- Requests should include complete business justification and relevant supporting data.
- Requests that do not contain security, compliance, or risk indicators may proceed directly to approval.
- If all validation checks are satisfied, the request may be forwarded for final approval.

## Decision Guidance

- Approve when: data is complete, valid, and no policy violations exist.
- Escalate when: missing or unclear information is present.
- Reject when: request clearly violates policy constraints.
