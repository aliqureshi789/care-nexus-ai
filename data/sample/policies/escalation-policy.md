# Escalation Policy

Reference: POL-ESC-003

Summary: Requests with missing data, incomplete information, or risk indicators must be escalated for further review.

## Description

This policy ensures that incomplete or high-risk requests are not processed without sufficient validation.

## Rules

- Requests missing required fields or documentation must not proceed.
- Any unclear or ambiguous request must be escalated.
- Requests that contain conflicting information must be reviewed manually.
- If risk indicators or policy ambiguity exists, escalate to the appropriate authority.

## Decision Guidance

- Escalate when: request is incomplete, unclear, or missing data.
- Approve when: missing information is resolved and validated.
- Reject when: request cannot be validated or corrected.
